The Missouri and the Yellowstone

The Missouri from its meeting with the Yellowstone to Williston and the head of Lake Sakakawea, drawn in lines alone, contours on the bluffs and waterlines stepping in from the shore. Drag to move, scroll to zoom, and right-drag or two-finger drag to tilt and turn.

Elevation: USGS 3D Elevation Program. Hydrography: USGS National Hydrography Dataset. Streets: North Dakota NG911 road centerlines.
