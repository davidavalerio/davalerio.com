The Basin, Exploded

Five surfaces of the Williston Basin, from the ground down to the Precambrian basement, cut as a core 196 miles across near McKenzie County, North Dakota, and lifted apart, with relief exaggerated 20 times. A plumb line from Watford City carries the depths.

Data: Gelman and Johnson, 2023, data release for the USGS three-dimensional petroleum systems model of the Williston Basin (doi:10.5066/P9N7O1OT).
